❓ FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about FlowSync AI.

What is FlowSync AI and how does it work?
+

FlowSync AI is an intelligent workflow automation platform that connects your apps and uses AI agents to automate repetitive, multi-step tasks. You create workflows by connecting triggers (events that start a workflow) to actions (things FlowSync does in response). Our AI layer adds intelligence — making decisions, enriching data, and adapting to context — so your automations aren't just rule-based, they're truly smart.

Do I need coding knowledge to use FlowSync?
+

Absolutely not. FlowSync was designed from the ground up for non-technical users. Our drag-and-drop visual canvas lets you build complex workflows without writing a single line of code. However, if you are a developer, we also offer a full REST API, webhooks, and code steps (JavaScript/Python) for when you need that extra flexibility.

Which apps does FlowSync integrate with?
+

FlowSync currently supports 500+ integrations including Slack, Notion, Salesforce, HubSpot, GitHub, Gmail, Google Sheets, Stripe, Airtable, Figma, Jira, Asana, Linear, Zoom, Dropbox, and hundreds more. We add new integrations every week based on user requests. If your tool isn't listed, you can connect it via our universal HTTP/webhook connector.

Is my data secure with FlowSync?
+

Security is our top priority. FlowSync is SOC 2 Type II certified, GDPR compliant, and HIPAA ready for enterprise customers. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We use isolated execution environments for workflows, never store your credentials in plaintext, and provide detailed audit logs. Enterprise customers also get private cloud deployment options.

Can I use AI agents on the free plan?
+

The free Starter plan includes access to basic AI features like AI-suggested workflow improvements and simple text processing. Full AI Agents — which can make autonomous decisions, browse the web, and run multi-step reasoning — are available starting from the Pro plan. Pro includes 5 AI Agent slots, while Enterprise offers unlimited custom agents with your own model fine-tuning.

What happens if I exceed my task limit?
+

We won't abruptly shut down your workflows. When you approach your task limit (at 80% and 100%), you'll receive email and in-app notifications. Your workflows will pause at the limit until the next billing cycle or until you upgrade. You can also purchase task top-ups at any time without upgrading your plan. We believe in transparency — no surprise charges, ever.
